DEBRIEFING

Thank you again for participating in our study: empirical research would not be possible without the help of participants such as yourself. We would hereby like to disclose the goal of the study and details related to its execution. You played a group game with other participants, in which you were asked to reach a consensus on which new product to launch (Tao or Eta). The goal of our study is to see the impact a minority of confederates countering the majority has on the speed with which the group reaches a consensus, depending on the consistency of the confederates. There were two conditions: in one, the confederates consistently countered the majority, while in the other some of the confederates switched between countering the majority and siding with it. The choices of the minority confederates were provided by a pre-programmed algorithm. We needed to resort to using confederates for the minority in order to make sure that a clear minority-majority dynamic emerges. Creating this dynamic allowed us to measure the difference in the speed of group consensus-reaching between different conditions. The bonus payoff you will receive will be based on the choices you and other participants made in the game, in the same way as described in the instructions at the beginning of the game. This concludes the debriefing. In case you have any additional questions or complaints about the study, you may contact the researcher at z.mlakar@rug.nl. Thanks again for your efforts!
