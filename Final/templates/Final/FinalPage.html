DEBRIEFING

Thank you again for participating in our study: empirical research would not be possible without the help of participants such as yourself. We hereby would like to disclose the goal of the study and details related to its execution. You played a group game with other participants, in which you were asked to reach a consensus on which new product to launch (Tao or Eta). The goal of our study is to see the impact a minority of confederates contradicting the majority has on the speed with which the group reaches a consensus, depending on the ratio of confederates present in the group. There were three confederate ratios varied between different groups, namely 17.6%, 25%, and 33.3%. The choices of the minority confederates were provided by a pre-programmed algorithm. We needed to resort to using confederates for the minority in order to make sure that clear minority-majority dynamic emerges. Creating this dynamic allowed us to measure the difference in the pace of group consensus-reaching between different conditions. The bonus payoff you will receive will be based on the choices you and other participants made in the game, in the same way as described in the instructions at the beginning of the game. The data gathered in this study will be used to compute parameters for an agent-based model, which we will use to try to predict social change outcomes under different conditions. This concludes the debriefing. In case you have any additional questions or complaints about the study, you may contact the researchers at z.mlakar@rug.nl. Thanks again for your efforts!
