Short instructions recap
Your task is to, in each round of the game, privately endorse one of the two New Products and observe how many and which individuals in your group endorsed each of the two New Products.

Goal of the game: As a group, release one of the two New Products by unanimously endorsing it with everyone in your group in a specific round.

Task rewards: You receive more bonus if
1) The New Product is released in earlier rounds of the game (if it does not get released at all, everyone in the group receives 0 bonus!),
2) You endorsed the released New Product relatively more often than others in your group.
